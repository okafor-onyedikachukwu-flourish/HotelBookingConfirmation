Our story

A yellow house above the harbour, since 1987.

My parents bought Maison Soleil when the shutters were falling off and the garden was mostly rosemary. Today there are four guest rooms, an olive tree older than all of us, and Poivre, who believes he owns the place.

Good to know

A few gentle house rules

  1. 01

    Quiet hours, 22:00 – 08:00

    Sound carries over the terrace at night. The garden is lovely for late conversations in low voices.

  2. 02

    Please don’t feed Poivre

    Whatever he tells you, he has already eaten. Twice.

  3. 03

    No smoking indoors

    There’s an ashtray on the terrace wall. Mind the dry garden in summer.

  4. 04

    Check-out by 11:00

    Leave your key on the hall table. Your bags can stay with us until the evening.
