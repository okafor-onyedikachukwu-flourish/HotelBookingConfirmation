Around town · Cassis

Margaux’s favourite corners.

Open in Maps

Cassis is small enough to walk everywhere. These are the places we send our friends. Times are on foot from our blue door unless we say otherwise.

Walk

01

Port de Cassis

6 min walk

Pastel houses, fishing boats and café terraces along the quay. It’s the heart of town, and where the calanque boats leave from.

Margaux’s tipCome back at golden hour for an apéritif facing the boats.

Food

02

Provençal market

Wednesday & Friday mornings · Place Baragnon

Olives, cheese, honey, flowers and fish, a few streets back from the port. Go hungry and say yes to the samples.

Margaux’s tipWednesday is your check-out day. Leave your bags with us and go.

Beach

03

Plage de la Grande Mer

7 min walk

The main town beach, right beside the harbour. Easy, lively and perfect for a quick swim before dinner.

Margaux’s tipBorrow a beach towel from the basket in the hall.

Beach

04

Plage du Bestouan

12 min walk

A small pebble beach just west of the port, with very clear water and a view back over the town.

Margaux’s tipBring water shoes. The pebbles mean business.

Walk

05

Calanque de Port-Miou

25 min walk

The nearest calanque: a long, narrow inlet of white limestone lined with sailboats. The trail carries on towards Port-Pin and En-Vau.

Margaux’s tipStart early on warm days. There’s little shade after 11.

Day trip

06

The calanques by boat

Boats leave from the harbour

See the cliffs and turquoise coves from the water. The easiest way to reach the calanques if you’d rather not hike.

Margaux’s tipBring a jacket. It’s always breezier out on the water.

Day trip

07

Cap Canaille

15 min drive · Route des Crêtes

A winding road along some of the highest sea cliffs in France, with huge views over Cassis and the bay.

Margaux’s tipThe road can close on very windy days. Ask us before you set off.

Getting around

Cassis train station sits about 3 km above the town, and a local bus runs down to the centre. Parking in the old town is tight, so leave the car at the house and walk.
