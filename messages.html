What time can we check in?

From 15:00 on Saturday 25 April. Ring the brass bell by the blue door. If we’re out, the key is in the terracotta pot by the olive tree.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, for two guests, every morning from 8:00 to 10:30 on the terrace. You can leave us a note on the Breakfast page.

Of course. Check-out is at 11:00, and your bags can stay in the hall until the evening.
